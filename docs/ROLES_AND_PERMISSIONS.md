# Roles and permissions

Foundation grants: `workspace:read`, `workspace:manage`, `team:read`, `team:manage`, `roles:read`, `roles:manage`, `audit:read`. Owner has all. Managing partner has read grants and team management; lawyer/researcher/paralegal/secretary/reception/accountant/finance/HR/document controller/auditor/client receive explicitly limited initial grants only. Legal and financial permissions will be introduced with their modules; no implicit `*` grants. Platform admin/support are not tenant roles.

Custom roles store validated catalog grants. Only owner can manage workspace settings and roles in the initial default policy. A custom role manager may only grant a subset of their current permissions. A team manager may not grant owner or any role with permissions above their own; cannot change an owner, themselves, or a member whose role exceeds their grant set. Role edits cannot indirectly elevate the actor. The reserved OWNER role and owner membership are immutable through these APIs. All actions are checked on the server and same-tenant branch/role references are checked in DB too.

Scopes: foundation membership branch is assigned and validated; branch record filtering and matter/document/client-level policy become mandatory in their respective modules. Initial team registry is office-wide for users with team:read; branch is organizational metadata here, not a claim of branch-restricted legal access. Client role has only workspace:read and no team/audit grants; portal is not yet exposed.

Future resources use View/Create/Edit/Delete/Approve/Print/Export/Share/Assign/Archive/Manage plus explicit finance/confidential flags. Lawyer default: assigned matters. Accountant: finance without secret drafts. Secretary: appointments without automatic case content. Client: own published documents and invoices. Support: metadata only; temporary authorized case access must have expiry/reason/consent/audit. Tests must use separate accounts for each role and explicit denials.
