# Implementation roadmap

0. Requirements, architecture, ERD, security, permissions, API, AI and operations design; preserve original request.
1. Foundation: monorepo, DB migrations, i18n, office registration, login/session, team/roles, isolation, TOTP MFA and password recovery with an interchangeable mail adapter. Implemented; final verification results and remaining release gates are in PROJECT_STATUS.md. Real email delivery remains disabled/unverified without provider settings. Existing-account invitations and richer profile/branch administration remain follow-up work.
2. Legal core implemented and tested: clients/courts/matters, assignment and branch ACL, hearings/tasks, daily/weekly/monthly calendar and task-state board, in-app reminders, conflict search and timeline. Advanced workflow/taxonomy/numbering/team/recurrence and calendar configuration/synchronization features remain; see LEGAL_OPERATIONS.md.
3. Private S3, scanning jobs, versions, text/OCR, drafts/templates, review/approval, PDF/DOCX export and print preview implemented and tested. Independent contracts/POAs/licences, obligations, expiry/renewal reminders and approved source-version pinning are implemented. Rich-text layout, standalone source-file intake and several DMS capabilities remain. Daily/weekly/monthly calendar and task-state board have been added; recurrence and external sync remain.
4. Invoices/receipts/expenses, fee/time entries, balanced journals, trial balance and separate client funds implemented and tested. Electronic payments/refunds/credit notes, recurring/multi-line billing, advanced reports and reconciliation remain.
5. AI provider configuration/consent, RAG/citations, legal assistance/research/drafting, quotas and AI analytics remain unimplemented. Working extraction/OCR is not the AI module.
6. Client portal implemented and tested: explicitly published cases/hearings/files/invoices, isolated messages/requests and immediate revocation. Upload intake, invitations, fulfillment and outbound notifications remain.
7. Platform: user promoted this ahead of the legal core on 2026-11-03. Implemented separate principals/MFA, firm/user metadata controls, plans/subscriptions/user quotas, manual platform invoices/receipts, published pages, brand/settings/country controls, administrator grants, sessions/audit and basic monitoring. Automated payments/refunds, support/break-glass, logo files, providers and full operational monitoring remain pending their integrations. See PLATFORM_ADMIN.md.
8. Release: role acceptance, security/performance, backup restore, UX, operations and Oman legal review.

Each stage requires working UI+API+DB, policy checks, tests and documentation. No stage is complete merely because routes or tables exist. Scope completion is reported per actual verification; outstanding gates are never hidden.
